# Program 8: K-Means Clustering on Iris Dataset

**Logic / Algorithm:** K-Means is an **unsupervised** machine learning algorithm used to group unlabeled data. 
1. You choose a number $K$ (how many groups you want).
2. The algorithm places $K$ random center points (centroids).
3. Every data point attaches to its closest centroid.
4. The centroids move to the true middle of their newly formed groups.
5. Repeat until the centroids stop moving.

**Iris Application:** The model is fed Iris flower measurements without being told the species. It successfully groups the flowers into 3 clusters just by observing their physical similarities!

**Real-World Example:** Marketing Customer Segmentation. A supermarket groups its millions of customers into 5 clusters based on purchasing habits (e.g., "Bargain Hunters", "Luxury Buyers") so they can send targeted coupons.
